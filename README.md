## Featured projects

> Four things I've built, from satellite imagery to groceries.

### 🛰️ ORBIS: Urban Sprawl Monitoring
Analyzes satellite imagery with deep learning to map and study how cities expand over time.
**Stack:** `Python` · `TensorFlow` · `FastAPI`

---

### 📝 SmartSummarizer: NLP Text Summarization
A head-to-head comparison of extractive (TextRank) and abstractive (transformer) summarization, evaluated with ROUGE.
**Stack:** `BERT` · `BART` · `T5` · `ROUGE`

---

### ⚡ Power Intelligence: Energy Monitoring System
Forecasts household power usage, flags abnormal consumption, and recommends ways to cut energy waste.
**Stack:** `Python` · `Scikit-learn` · `Flask`

---

### 🛒 Quick Kart: Grocery Shopping App
A clean grocery storefront with categories, product filtering, and a working cart.
**Stack:** `HTML` · `CSS` · `JavaScript` · `Flask`
